About the academy

A home for cricket in Bamutia

360° Cricket Academy is a cricket coaching academy at Chuto Aamtail, Bamutia, in West Tripura. We welcome children, teenagers and adults, whether you are holding a bat for the first time or preparing for serious competition.

Every student is coached on technique, mindset and fitness. Batches are arranged by age, so players train alongside peers and get attention that suits their stage.

Programs

Six ways we sharpen your game

Pick a single discipline or train across all of them. Each program is delivered by session, with feedback after every drill.

01

Batting

Build a solid defence, then learn to score all around the ground.

  • Grip, stance and balance
  • Front and back foot play
  • Net sessions and shot selection
02

Bowling

Develop a clean action with control over line, length and variation.

  • Pace and spin fundamentals
  • Accuracy drills
  • Action correction
03

Fielding

Sharper hands, quicker feet and confident throws from every position.

  • Catching and ground fielding
  • Throwing accuracy
  • Agility and reaction work
04

Wicketkeeping

Specialist training for the most demanding role on the field.

  • Stance and glove work
  • Standing up and standing back
  • Stumpings and run outs
05

Fitness

Conditioning that builds strength, stamina and injury resilience.

  • Strength and mobility
  • Speed and endurance
  • Cricket specific conditioning
06

Age-wise Batches

Training groups matched to age, so every player learns at the right pace.

  • Junior, youth and adult groups
  • Level-based drills
  • Practice matches
